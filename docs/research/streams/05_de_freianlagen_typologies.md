# Stream 05 – German open-space (Freianlagen) object catalogs and technical typologies of vegetation and surfaces

Research date: 2026-09-30. Status: **final for this stream** (remaining gaps are listed under "Open points").

Evidence marks used in every table:

- **V** = verified by me this session in a primary source (publisher / standards body / law text, or the publisher's own free sample pages and tables of contents).
- **S** = secondary source read this session (vendor documentation, municipal or state guide, trade press, lecture notes, Wikipedia).
- **R** = recalled from professional background knowledge, **not verified this session** – treat as a hypothesis to be checked before it becomes catalog metadata.

Method notes. DIN and FLL full texts are paywalled; I used official abstracts, tables of contents and FLL's free "Inhaltsübersicht" sample PDFs, plus vendor/municipal documents that reproduce excerpts. The session-wide web-search budget ran out part-way through this stream, so sections 6–8 lean more on R than I would like; nothing marked R has a source URL. No codes, numbers or definitions were invented; truncated labels are shown truncated.

### 1.3 Object-type list – what is publicly documented

**Read this first.** Every code I could find publicly belongs to the **2016/2018 numbering (basis DIN 276-1:2008-12: 520 Befestigte Flächen, 530 Baukonstruktionen, 540 Technische Anlagen, 550 Einbauten, 560 Wasserflächen, 570 Pflanz- und Saatflächen)**. The **OK FREI 2025 numbering (basis DIN 276:2018-12: 530 Oberbau/Deckschichten, 540 Baukonstruktionen, 550 Technische Anlagen, 560 Einbauten, 570 Vegetationsflächen, 580 Wasserflächen) is not publicly documented** – I could not verify a single 2025 code. Section 2.2 gives the concordance of the first three digits only.

Provenance keys: **[G]** GALK Leitfaden GFMS 2024 – reproduces genuine OK FREI 2018 rows and a Rostock catalog "gem. OK FREI 2018"; **[W]** d.b.g. Werksnorm Leseprobe 2019 – table of contents and figure "Auszug aus der Signaturenbibliothek der FLL für Freianlagen (SK-FREI)"; **[L]** d.b.g. product sheet "Luftbildauswertung – Erfassungsvarianten" ("Systematik … des OK FREI der FLL"); **[Z]** d.b.g. "Der Lebenszyklus von Außenanlagen" (d.b.g. Deutschland-Katalog, Stand März 2016, "Flächeninhalt nach OK FREI" plus own "Profile"). Codes whose last digit is not 0 (x.yy.**z**) are d.b.g. detail levels, not FLL. Labels ending in "…" were truncated in the source figure and are deliberately **not completed**.

### 3.3 Rasen – Blumenrasen/Kräuterrasen – Wiese – Blumenwiese/Blühwiese – Blühstreifen

None of these boundaries is fixed in a single standard; DIN 18919 only speaks of "Rasen und wiesenähnliche Flächen" (V, table of contents) and the OK FREI lumps "Wiese (Landschaftsrasen, Blumenwiese)". The distinction used in practice is mowing frequency, sward height and species composition:

### 3.4 Regional seed: § 40 BNatSchG and the FLL recommendations

§ 40 Abs. 1 BNatSchG (V, https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bnatschg_2009/__40.html ; also quoted in FLL RSM 2026): "Das Ausbringen von Pflanzen in der freien Natur, deren Art in dem betreffenden Gebiet in freier Natur nicht oder seit mehr als 100 Jahren nicht mehr vorkommt, sowie von Tieren bedarf der Genehmigung der zuständigen Behörde. Dies gilt nicht für künstlich vermehrte Pflanzen, wenn sie ihren genetischen Ursprung in dem betreffenden Gebiet haben. …" The transition period ended on **1 March 2020** (V, FLL RSM 2026 foreword). The technical reference is the FLL "Empfehlungen für Begrünungen mit gebietseigenem Saatgut", **1. Ausgabe Mai 2014** (still the edition on sale; V, https://shop.fll.de/de/downloadable/download/sample/sample_id/115/): it defines *gebietseigenes Saatgut* with the two qualities **Regiosaatgut** (seed propagated within one of **22 Ursprungsgebiete (UG)**, e.g. UG 01 Nordwestdeutsches Tiefland, with regionalised mixtures **RSM Regio 1–22**) and **naturraumtreues Saatgut** (material harvested from donor sites and transferred, e.g. as mown material or threshed seed), and covers ErMiV (Erhaltungsmischungsverordnung), tendering and acceptance. DIN 18917:2018-07 has its own clause 5.4 "Gebietseigenes Saatgut" (V). FLL states that "die Regel-Saatgut-Mischungen der RSM Rasen sind nicht für Begrünungen mit gebietseigenem ('autochthonem') Saatgut geeignet" and ZTV La-StB 18 allows RSM only "wenn eine Begrünung mit gebietseigenem Saatgut nicht erforderlich ist" (V, RSM 2026). Whether an inner-urban park counts as "freie Natur" is a legal question handled differently by the Länder (R).

## Implications for the element catalog

Design consequences first:

- **Carry two code fields per element, each with its edition**: `din276_2018` (three-digit KG, stable and verified) and `okfrei_2018` (FLL/d.b.g. code in the old numbering, secondary-source quality). Add `okfrei_2025` as an empty field to be filled once the 2025 Excel file is available. Never store a bare "574" without edition.
- **The current 16 classes are too coarse on three axes that German practice always distinguishes**: lawn type (Zier-, Gebrauchs-, Strapazier-, Landschaftsrasen, Wiese, Blumenwiese), planting type (Stauden, Wechselflor, Rosen, Bodendecker, Strauchfläche, Formhecke, freiwachsende Hecke) and paving material × bond (Naturstein / Betonstein / Klinker / Platten).
- **Name mapping of the existing classes**: Lawn → Gebrauchsrasen; Meadow → Wiese (Landschaftsrasen); Wildflower meadow → Blumenwiese; Shrub → Strauchfläche; Woodland → Baumbestandsfläche (Gehölzbestand); Urban trees → Einzelbaum (Straßenbaum / Anlagenbaum); Reed/wetland → Röhricht / Flachwasserzone; Water body → Stehendes Gewässer / Fließgewässer; Soil/bare ground → Offener Boden; Sand → Sandfläche (Spielsand, Fallschutzsand); Paving (light) → Betonstein-/Plattenbelag; Paving (dark) → Natursteinpflaster (or Pflasterklinker); Asphalt → Asphaltfläche; Concrete → Betonfläche; Gravel → Kies-/Splittfläche (distinct from wassergebundene Wegedecke, which is missing today); Wood decking → Holzfläche (Holzdeck).
- **Colour families** should stay compatible with the FLL SK FREI logic users already know (grey pavings, sand-yellow play surfaces, turquoise water, greens for vegetation with pink/magenta for beds, red for buildings), rendered in the pastel house style.
- Texture motifs below assume tiles designed for 1:200–1:500 display with a second, more literal variant for 1:50–1:100.

Codes: KG = DIN 276:2018-12 (S-verified); OKF = OK FREI 2016/2018 numbering incl. d.b.g. extensions (S); "–" = none found.
